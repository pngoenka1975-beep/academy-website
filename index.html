Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our admission process and teaching methods.

Which classes do you offer courses for?

We offer dedicated foundation batches for Classes 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, and 10th, alongside target JEE & NEET courses for Class 11th, 12th, and Dropper students.

What is the maximum batch size at Rising Starz?

To maintain our strict 1:15 teacher-to-student attention ratio, classroom batches are capped at 35–40 students maximum.

Are daily doubt clearing counters included in the batch fee?

Yes! Daily 1-on-1 doubt resolution counters (8 AM – 8 PM) are completely free for all enrolled classroom and hybrid students.
