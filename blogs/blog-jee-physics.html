IIT-JEE Advanced Physics: Mechanics & Electrodynamics

By Rising Starz Mentors • 10 min read • Updated January 2026

Physics is often the most challenging section of JEE Advanced. This guide covers Mechanics and Electrodynamics — two of the highest-weightage units — with concept breakdowns, solved examples, and previous year questions.

1. Mechanics — The Foundation

Mechanics forms the backbone of JEE Physics. Approximately 25–30% of physics questions come from this unit.

  • Key Topics: Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work-Energy-Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, Oscillations, Waves.
  • Focus Area: Constraint relations, pseudo forces, rolling motion, and fluid mechanics.
  • Strategy: Master Newton's laws before attempting rotational mechanics. Draw free-body diagrams for every problem.

2. Electrodynamics — High Scoring Unit

Electrodynamics includes electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism, and electromagnetic induction.

  • Key Topics: Coulomb's law, electric field, Gauss's law, capacitors, Kirchhoff's laws, magnetic force, EMI, AC circuits.
  • Focus Area: Capacitor circuits, LR/CR/AC circuits, and magnetic dipole moment.
  • Strategy: Practice circuit analysis daily. Memorize derivations for field due to common charge distributions.

3. Previous Year Questions Practice

Solving JEE Advanced PYQs from the last 10 years is essential. Questions often repeat concepts with slight variations.

  • Solve Paper 1 and Paper 2 separately under timed conditions.
  • Focus on integer-type and matrix-match questions.
  • Analyze mistakes — categorize them as conceptual, calculation, or time-management errors.
