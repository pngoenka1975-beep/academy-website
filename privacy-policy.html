Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Introduction

Rising Starz ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at risingstarz.example.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our site or submit an inquiry through our contact form.

Information We Collect

When you fill out our Contact Us form, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Target exam / course interest
  • Message / question
  • Timestamp of submission

We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as health, biometric, or government ID information) through this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your admissions inquiry
  • To share course, batch, and scholarship information
  • To improve our services and communications

Data Storage & Security

Submitted inquiries are recorded in a Google Sheet via Google Apps Script. Access is restricted to authorized Rising Starz staff only. We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data, but no internet transmission is 100% secure.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us (details below).

Cookies & Tracking

Our site uses analytics and CDN services (Tailwind CSS, FontAwesome, Google Fonts, Leaflet) that may set cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
